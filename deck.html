Concertina V truss deck

One big double sheet, taped with Warren truss webs side by side and folded like a concertina along the chords. Unfolded, the webs lean alternately left and right, and neighbouring webs share their fold chords. Pumped full, it becomes a space truss.

Model notes

One V module (two webs, a whole number of bays) is simulated. Its cut edges are symmetry planes standing in for the neighbours, and it is repeated over the footprint. The concrete weight acts through the hydrostatic pressure. The cloth has no bending stiffness.
