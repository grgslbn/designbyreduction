Cut list

Sewing lines for each part type with how many of each. Columns, tubes and walls are sewn into tubes and stitched to each other at the feet.

Model notes

Each part type is simulated once and repeated over the layout. Contact between neighbours (walls pressing on each other, tubes merging into feet) is not modelled yet. Columns, tubes and walls are filled in one pour through the necks. The slab is poured later and loads only the panels.
